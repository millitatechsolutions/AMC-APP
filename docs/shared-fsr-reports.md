# Shared FSR reports for UBL and PowerVision

The assigned technician submits one FSR revision. Authorized UBL Office and PowerVision Head Office accounts read that same record, including all 225 fields, paper/before/after evidence, consumed materials, actor identity, timestamps, immutable revisions and branch decisions. There is no manually copied client version.

## Use the reports

1. PowerVision registers and activates the technician account, then assigns an untouched service visit. The technician signs in to their own portal and submits the completed FSR.
2. In PowerVision, open **FSR reports** and select the report.
3. In UBL Office, open the same submitted branch report. Authorized UBL users receive the same recorded details and images.
4. Compare the completed paper image with the structured entries. Each organization can record its own manual Matched/Mismatch decision for the exact revision.
5. The matching UBL branch reviewer can acknowledge or request corrections with a reason. Head-office manual verification is separate from branch acknowledgment.
6. Inspect previous immutable submissions through the revision selector. Browser printing can save a PDF where supported.

The tracked materials section includes only the items/quantities used in that FSR. Company stock balances, receipts and movement history remain in PowerVision's Inventory screen and API; UBL cannot access that register.

A new corrected submission needs a new company/client comparison, while previous checks remain attached to earlier revisions. Stock changes only by the correction's net quantity difference. Starting or drafting does not count as performed; submission does. Branch acknowledgment changes payment eligibility without double-counting the visit.

Accounts, portals, client ownership, company ownership, branch assignment and technician assignment are checked by the server. Private pages and downloads require the correct session. Unknown or foreign-client records remain excluded. Earlier mistaken HBL demo records are preserved for history but hidden from registered portals.
